What this module does

Subscription Management adds a complete recurring-billing engine to Odoo. A subscription is simply a confirmed sales order that carries a recurring plan, so it reuses Odoo's own invoicing, taxes and accounting and every total stays correct. Pick a plan in the order header, confirm the quotation, and the order starts billing on its interval.

A daily job then bills every subscription that is due, optionally charges a saved payment token and reconciles it, and pauses billing with a clear warning if a charge fails. Customers can view, pause and close their subscription from the portal, while managers watch monthly recurring revenue, churn and renewals on an interactive dashboard where every tile drills into the records behind it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set up recurring billing and subscriptions in Odoo?

Open a quotation, pick a recurring plan in the order header, and confirm it. The order becomes a subscription that bills itself on the plan's interval, using Odoo's own invoicing and accounting so the numbers stay correct.

Does it charge the customer's card automatically each period?

Yes. Save a payment token on the subscription and the daily job charges it for each recurring invoice and reconciles the payment. If a charge fails, the subscription is flagged and billing pauses until you retry or the customer updates their method.

Can customers manage their own subscription?

Yes. From the customer portal they can view their subscription and next invoice date, and pause or close it when you allow those actions on the plan. Each permission is controlled per plan.

What happens when a subscription payment fails?

The subscription is marked with a payment exception and a banner explains that billing is paused. You can retry in one click, and if the oldest unpaid invoice stays overdue past the plan's grace period a scheduled job closes the subscription and records it as a payment-failed churn.

Is there an MRR and churn dashboard?

Yes. The command center shows monthly recurring revenue, active and paused counts, new and churned in the period, churn rate, expansion, ARPU and projected revenue, a status swimlane, and six charts. Every tile, lane and chart is clickable and opens the underlying records.
